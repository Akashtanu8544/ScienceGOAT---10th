1. Introduction / परिचय

This Privacy Policy explains how Science GOAT - 10th Science RBSE (Package ID: com.sciencegoat.class10rbse) handles data when you use our Android mobile application and Progressive Web Application (PWA).

यह गोपनीयता नीति यह स्पष्ट करती है कि जब आप हमारे कक्षा 10वीं विज्ञान ऐप का उपयोग करते हैं, तो डेटा का प्रबंधन किस प्रकार किया जाता है। हमारा मुख्य उद्देश्य विद्यार्थियों को एक सुरक्षित, पारदर्शी और विज्ञापन-मुक्त/हितैषी शिक्षण वातावरण प्रदान करना है।

2. Data Collection and Usage / डेटा संग्रह एवं उपयोग

We strictly adhere to Google Play's User Data Policy. Here is how data is managed in our app:

  • Personal Information (व्यक्तिगत जानकारी): We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or precise device identifiers.
  • Local Study Progress (स्थानीय शिक्षण प्रगति): Your chapter progress, test scores, bookmarked questions, and theme preferences (Dark/Light mode) are saved locally on your device using standard browser LocalStorage and IndexedDB APIs. This data never leaves your device unless you manually back it up or sync it via your own custom settings.
  • Offline PDF & Asset Caching (ऑफलाइन नोट्स कैशिंग): The app utilizes a Progressive Web App Service Worker to cache study material (handwritten notes, syllabus PDFs, model papers) directly onto your device so you can study offline without an active internet connection.

3. Permissions Requested / आवश्यक अनुमतियाँ

Science GOAT asks only for essential technical capabilities required for app functionality:

  • Internet Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to fetch latest study material, PDF notes, video lecture embeds, and model test papers.
  • Storage / Service Worker Cache (LOCAL_STORAGE): Used to store offline study materials and save quiz scores locally.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) [Optional]: Used solely to send occasional study reminders, daily science tips, and exam countdown alerts. You can disable this at any time in your device settings.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Student Safety) / बच्चों एवं विद्यार्थियों की गोपनीयता

Science GOAT is an educational study application designed for secondary school students (RBSE Class 10th, generally ages 13-18). We take children's privacy very seriously:

  • We do NOT solicit or collect personal data from children under 13 or any age group.
  • We do NOT include any adult, harmful, or inappropriate content.
  • All study notes, video tutorials, and question banks are strictly vetted for academic appropriateness under the Rajasthan Board (RBSE) & NCERT curriculum.

5. Third-Party Services / तृतीय-पक्ष सेवाएं

To deliver video lectures and ensure app stability, the app may integrate standard third-party libraries:

  • Google Play Services: Required for fundamental app framework operations on Android devices.
  • YouTube Embedded Player: Video lectures are embedded using official YouTube Player APIs to play educational videos hosted publicly. YouTube may collect non-personal viewing telemetry in accordance with Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security & Retention / डेटा सुरक्षा एवं प्रतिधारण

Because all learning progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, you remain in complete control of your data:

  • Data Retention: Data persists on your device as long as the application remains installed.
  • Data Deletion / Reset: You can wipe all saved test scores and offline caches at any time by going to App Settings → Reset Progress or by clearing the application storage in your Android system settings. Uninstalling the app completely removes all locally stored app data.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory compliance. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
